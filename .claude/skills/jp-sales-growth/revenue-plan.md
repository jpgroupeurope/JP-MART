# Starter revenue plan: €15,000 → €30,000 / month

Assumptions (replace with real numbers): average first order ≈ €1,500; 1 in 3 qualified conversations becomes an order; positive reply to cold email ≈ 3–4%; repeat customers reorder every 4–6 weeks.

## Funnel maths (month 1–3 target €15k, then €30k)
| Step | €15k/month | €30k/month |
|---|---|---|
| Orders needed (new + reorders) | 10 | 20 |
| Qualified conversations (≈1 in 3 close) | 30 | 60 |
| A/B-tier contacts to reach (≈3–4% positive) | ~900 | ~1,800 |
| Emails/day (22 working days, 4-touch sequence) | ~10–15 | ~25–40 |
Reorders carry growth: 10 accounts reordering monthly at €1,500 = €15k without new leads. Prioritise getting **repeat buyers**, not one-off orders.

## Market order (no lawyer available → lowest legal risk first)
1. **Netherlands, France**: cold B2B email allowed with relevance + opt-out. Start here.
2. **Germany, Spain, Italy, Poland**: do NOT cold-email or cold-WhatsApp. Use phone calls, LinkedIn, trade fairs, inbound (website form, Google/LinkedIn ads), referrals, and email only to people who asked or already bought.
3. Add countries one at a time after the first 5 orders in the previous one.

## Without a lawyer (minimum safe setup)
- Keep a written lawful-basis note per segment (why this business would want this product).
- Email: identify the company, give a one-click unsubscribe, honour opt-outs the same day, keep a suppression list. Target company/role addresses only.
- WhatsApp: opt-in only, store proof of consent.
- Consider a one-off paid GDPR/marketing-law check (a few hundred euro) before scaling past ~500 contacts per country; some chambers of commerce and trade bodies offer cheap advice.

## 30-day plan
- Week 1: pick 2 product lines + 1 country (NL or FR); write price list (MOQ, tier prices, delivery time); build 200 A/B leads; set up sending domain (SPF/DKIM/DMARC) and start warm-up.
- Week 2: send sequence to first 100–150 leads; call every positive reply within 24h; ship samples.
- Week 3: second batch; WhatsApp only for opted-in replies; follow-up calls on samples.
- Week 4: review KPIs, keep the best segment/subject, convert sample receivers to trial orders, set reorder reminders.

## Weekly KPI sheet
Contacts sent · bounce % · replies · positive replies · samples sent · quotes · orders · € revenue · reorder % · cost per order. Review every Monday; compare to the funnel table above.

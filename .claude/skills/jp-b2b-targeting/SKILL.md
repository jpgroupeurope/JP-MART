# JP B2B Targeting

## Step 1 - Define the ICP (one per segment)
| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Country / region | Germany – NRW |
| Buyer type | Ethnic/Asian grocery chain (5–30 stores) |
| Product fit | Rice, noodles, sauces, snacks |
| Size | 5–200 staff, €1–50M turnover |
| Decision maker | Purchasing manager / owner / category buyer |
| Buying trigger | New store opening, supplier change, seasonal demand, new product category |
| Disqualifiers | Exclusive supply contracts, discounters with central listing only, no food licence |

Segments to consider per country: ethnic/specialty grocery, independent supermarkets, cash & carry, HoReCa wholesalers, convenience/kiosk chains, importers/distributors of food & general goods, online grocers, buying groups/cooperatives (e.g. DE, FR, IT have many).

## Step 2 - Sources (legitimate only)
- Company registries: Handelsregister/Bundesanzeiger (DE), Infogreffe/Pappers (FR), Registro Imprese (IT), Registro Mercantil/Einforma (ES), KvK (NL), KRS/CEIDG (PL).
- Trade bodies & fair exhibitor/visitor lists, chamber of commerce directories (IHK, CCI, Camera di Commercio, Cámaras, KvK, KIG).
- Google Maps / OpenStreetMap for store locations, LinkedIn Sales Navigator, Kompass, Europages, Apollo/Lusha-type tools (check their GDPR terms).
- Existing customers, enquiries, website forms, referrals (warmest).
- Do NOT scrape personal data against site terms, buy dubious "1M EU emails" lists, or use private mobile numbers without lawful basis.

## Step 3 - Prefer role-based, company-level contacts
B2B GDPR is safer with role/company addresses (purchasing@, info@, einkauf@) plus named decision-makers found on the company's own public professional pages. Record **source + date + lawful basis** for every contact.

## Step 4 - Score (A/B/C)
Score 0–100: product fit 30, size/volume potential 20, decision-maker identified 15, trigger present 15, reachable channel & lawful basis 10, geography/logistics 10. **A ≥ 70, B 50–69, C < 50 (no outreach).**

## Step 5 - Lead record template
company, legal form, VAT/ID, country, city, website, buyer type, product fit, size, contact name, role, email, phone, WhatsApp opt-in (Y/N + date), source, lawful basis, score, language, status, last touch, next step, owner.

## Step 6 - Hygiene
Deduplicate by VAT/domain; validate emails (syntax, MX, catch-all risk) before send; remove bounces immediately; keep suppression list (unsubscribes, objections) and check against it every send; refresh data every 6 months; delete on request within 30 days.

## Language
Match local language: de, fr, it, es, nl, pl (English only for importers/international buyers). Never machine-translate without native review for first contact.

## Output
Return: ICP table, source plan, scored lead sheet (CSV columns above), recommended channel per lead, and counts per tier.

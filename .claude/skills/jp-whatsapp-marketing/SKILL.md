# JP WhatsApp Marketing

## Hard rules
- **Opt-in only.** Meta's policy and EU law (GDPR/ePrivacy) require consent before marketing messages. No cold WhatsApp blasts, no bought numbers.
- Use **WhatsApp Business Platform (API)** for broadcasts; the normal app/groups risk bans.
- Marketing outside the 24h customer window requires an approved **template**. Quality rating drops (blocks/reports) reduce limits - protect it.
- Every campaign includes a clear "reply STOP to unsubscribe" and an honoured suppression list.
- Business-owner mobile numbers are personal data: store source, date, consent text.

## How to get opt-ins (warm first)
- After email reply, call, trade-fair meeting, sample request, website form checkbox ("Send me price lists on WhatsApp").
- Click-to-WhatsApp link/QR on catalogue PDFs, email signatures, packaging, invoices, delivery notes.
- Existing customers: ask during order confirmation.

## Use cases and templates (localise to de/fr/it/es/nl/pl)
1. **Welcome / sample follow-up**: "Hello {Name}, this is {Sender} from JP Mart. Thanks for your interest - your sample of {product} ships {date}. Want our price list for {country}?"
2. **Price list / catalogue**: weekly or bi-weekly PDF + 3 hero products + MOQ + delivery window.
3. **Reorder reminder**: sent ~80% into the customer's usual reorder cycle: "Your last order of {product} was {x} weeks ago. Reorder the same? Reply YES."
4. **Stock/promo alert**: limited stock, new arrival, seasonal (Ramadan, Christmas, Easter, summer).
5. **Order/delivery updates** (utility templates - cheapest, highest trust).
6. **Re-activation**: 60–90 days inactive, personalised offer.

## Message style
≤60 words, one CTA, one product focus, a name, local language, no spammy caps/emoji overload. Use quick-reply buttons (Yes / Price list / Call me).

## Frequency
Max 2–4 marketing messages/month per contact; send Tue–Thu, 09:00–12:00 local time. Respect country holidays.

## Segments
Only message contacts in tier A/B from `jp-b2b-targeting` with opt-in recorded. Separate lists by country, buyer type and product category.

## Handling replies
Reply within 15 min in business hours; hand to sales for quotes; log in CRM; offer a call for orders above threshold.

## KPIs
Delivery %, read %, reply %, button click %, opt-out % (<1%), block rate, orders/revenue per 100 messages sent.

## Output
Deliver: segment, opt-in evidence, template text(s) in local language + English, send schedule, KPIs, compliance checklist.
